# Axes

Each axis reads every facts file and the bible, and applies every check of
its section. A check finds nothing until it has been run on every chapter.

## characters

Sections read: Personnages, Révélations, Corps; `bible/characters/`.

- **Attributes**: a physical or identity fact stated twice differently, or
  against the sheet outside its `{dès}`/`{jusqu'à}` range (`text-vs-text`,
  `text-vs-bible`).
- **Knowledge**: a character acting on, or stating as known, something the
  facts show they learn later or never (`knowledge`). Build each main
  character's "learns at" list from the facts first.
- **Skills**: a character doing what their savoir-faire excludes or above
  its level, with no learning in between (`skill`).
- **Reveals**: the text stating a `{secret}`, or a `{révélé chN}` fact
  before chN (`reveal-leak`).
- **Relations and address**: tutoiement/vouvoiement and the nature of a
  relation changing without a scene that changes it (`text-vs-text`).
- **Ages and dates**: an age that does not follow the timeline.

## situations

Sections read: Temps, Lieux et déplacements, Objets, Corps; the timeline,
place sheets (distances and trajets), the calendar system if any.

Build first the story's chronology: each chapter's start and end on one
time axis, from the Temps facts and the timeline.

- **Time**: durations that do not add up, a day that has two nights,
  seasons or moon phases against the calendar (`timeline`).
- **Presence**: a character in two places at once, or present in a scene
  they left without coming back (`presence`).
- **Travel**: a journey shorter than the distances table allows for its
  means of travel (`travel`).
- **Objects**: an object used after being lost, given, destroyed, or in two
  hands at once (`object`).
- **Bodies**: a wound, illness or fatigue that disappears faster than it
  can heal, changes side, or is forgotten (`body`).

## world

Sections read: Monde, Lieux et déplacements; `bible/world/`, the lexicon.

- **Places**: a description against the place's sheet or its parent's
  (climate, size, government) (`geography`).
- **Systems**: a use beyond its rules, without its cost, by someone
  without access, or an exception never set up (`rule`). Statements
  matching a `{croyance}` are not contradictions.
- **Spelling**: a name outside the lexicon's term and variants
  (`spelling`).
- **Reveals**: world `{secret}`s stated in the text (`reveal-leak`).
